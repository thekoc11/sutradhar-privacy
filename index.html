Sutradhar — Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Sutradhar (“we”, “us”) builds and hosts digital wedding-invitation pages and an RSVP service (“the Service”). This policy explains what data we collect when you receive or interact with a Sutradhar invitation, and how we handle it.

What we collect

  • Invitation response details you provide: your name, phone number, whether you will attend, number of guests, and any notes you choose to add (for example, dietary preferences).
  • Basic technical data required to deliver the invitation, such as message-delivery status through the messaging platform.

How we use it

  • To record and manage event attendance (RSVP) and to share it with the hosts of the event you were invited to.
  • To send you replies and updates related to that event, such as a confirmation of your RSVP.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where your data lives

  • RSVP responses are stored in a private database managed by us on behalf of the event hosts.
  • Messages are delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta. Meta’s own terms and privacy policy apply to the transport of messages.

Retention

RSVP data is kept until shortly after the event it relates to, after which it is deleted or archived at the hosts’ request.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your RSVP at any time by messaging the invitation number or writing to the contact below.
